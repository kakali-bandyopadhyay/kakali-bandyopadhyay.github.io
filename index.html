Process miningSynthetic data

Access-flow mining: approver or handoff bottleneck?

Problem Slow access provisioning gets blamed on approvers, but the delay can sit in cross-region handoffs and rework instead.

What it does Mines a simulated access-provisioning workflow across three regions; a pre-registered rule classifies delay as approver- or handoff-bound, cross-checked with PM4Py conformance. Includes AI triage behind a human approval gate, a UiPath intake-validation bot and a payback model.

Evidence Pre-registered classifier correct in all 4 test cells; the p95 band miss is reported, not tuned away. 113 tests. The gate fails closed, blocks self-approval and needs two approvers for privileged requests. Synthetic data.

How I build

  • Narrow first release, explicit exclusions and a trade-off record for every scope decision.
  • People make the decisions; the system prepares evidence, checks controls and records who decided what.
  • Evaluation reported per run and per question, including the misses; held-out scores are never combined or re-scored after fixes.
  • Independent review against a definition-of-done list before anything is published.

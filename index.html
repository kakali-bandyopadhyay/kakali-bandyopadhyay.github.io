DataQuant researchSQS · Lambda · DynamoDBPublic data

Volatility forecasting lab: pre-registered study and data/AI platform

Problem Does VIX-implied variance add anything to a HAR forecast of the next 30 days' S&P 500 variance – and can the answer, and the FOMC text behind it, be served by a platform that proves every number?

What it does A pre-registered walk-forward study (HAR, HAR-X, GARCH, MLP) plus an FOMC document pipeline through Kafka, an AWS serverless lake, Spark, hybrid OpenSearch + Qdrant search and a Redis cache; a LangGraph agent that may only repeat computed numbers; MLflow, drift monitoring, an Airflow DAG and a Kubernetes (kind) deployment.

Evidence Clark–West p = 0.0009 outside Feb–Apr 2020 (not detected on the full sample, p = 0.0597) · fused retrieval recall@5 0.589 vs dense 0.389 on 15 frozen questions; cloud path equal to local (266/266 docs, 6,620 chunks) · agent 0.844 (38/45), no unverified number in any answer, USD 0.064 for 45 runs. Public data; per-date licensed series not published.

Access-flow mining: approver or handoff bottleneck?

Problem Slow access provisioning gets blamed on approvers, but the delay can sit in cross-region handoffs and rework instead.

What it does Mines a simulated access-provisioning workflow across three regions; a pre-registered rule classifies delay as approver- or handoff-bound, cross-checked with PM4Py conformance. Includes AI triage behind a human approval gate, a UiPath intake-validation bot and a payback model.

How I build

  • Narrow first release, explicit exclusions and a trade-off record for every scope decision.
  • People make the decisions; the system prepares evidence, checks controls and records who decided what.
  • Evaluation reported per run and per question, including the misses; held-out scores are never combined or re-scored after fixes.
  • Independent review against a definition-of-done list before anything is published.
